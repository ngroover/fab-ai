 COMPLETE (implemented + unit tested)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [x] Bare Fangs   (Card::BareFangsR)
        Draw a card then discard a card; if the discarded card has 6 or more
        power, this attack gets +2 power.
        Tests: bare_fangs_discarding_power6_grants_plus2_power,
               bare_fangs_discarding_below_power6_grants_no_bonus

  [x] Wild Ride    (Card::WildRideR)
        Draw a card then discard a card; if the discarded card has 6 or more
        power, this attack gains Go Again.
        Tests: wild_ride_discarding_power6_grants_go_again,
               wild_ride_discarding_below_power6_grants_no_go_again

  [x] Alpha Rampage (Card::AlphaRampageR)
        As an additional cost to play, discard a card. Legal-action gates only
        offer it (and the pitches paying for it) when the hand can both cover
        the cost and keep a card back to discard; the discard (a random hand
        card) is paid as the card is played.
        Tests: discard_cost_card_unplayable_when_paying_leaves_no_card_to_discard,
               discard_cost_card_playable_with_a_spare_card_to_discard,
               discard_cost_card_playable_when_floating_resources_cover_the_gap,
               pitch_for_discard_cost_card_excludes_the_card_needed_to_discard,
               discard_cost_card_discards_a_hand_card_when_played

  [x] Wrecker Romp  (Card::WreckerRompB)
        As an additional cost to play, discard a card. Shares the discard-cost
        legal-action gating and play-time discard with Alpha Rampage.
        Tests: discard_cost_card_unplayable_when_paying_leaves_no_card_to_discard,
               discard_cost_card_playable_with_a_spare_card_to_discard

  [x] Awakening Bellow (Card::AwakeningBellowR)
        Go Again, Intimidate. On play, the next attack with the brute type you
        play this turn gets +3 power. The bonus is banked on the player and
        applied only to a brute attack, so a non-brute attack in between does
        not consume it.
        Tests: awakening_bellow_has_go_again_and_intimidate,
               awakening_bellow_banks_plus3_for_the_next_brute_attack,
               awakening_bellow_followup_brute_attack_gets_plus3,
               awakening_bellow_followup_generic_attack_does_not_get_plus3

  [x] Rhinar (hero)  (Card::Rhinar)
        Constant ability (OnDiscard6Intimidate): whenever Rhinar discards a card
        with 6 or more power, Intimidate (the opponent banishes a card from
        hand). Fires from both discard paths — the "discard a card" additional
        cost (Alpha Rampage, Wrecker Romp) and the play-effect draw-then-discard
        (Bare Fangs, Wild Ride). Independent of the played card's own keywords,
        so Alpha Rampage (Intimidate) discarding a 6-power card intimidates twice.
        Tests: rhinar_constant_ability_is_on_discard6_intimidate,
               discard_cost_of_power6_card_triggers_intimidate,
               discard_cost_of_sub_power6_card_does_not_trigger_intimidate,
               bare_fangs_play_effect_power6_discard_also_intimidates,
               non_rhinar_hero_power6_discard_does_not_intimidate,
               alpha_rampage_power6_discard_intimidates_twice

  [x] Pack Hunt    (Card::PackHuntR)
        Brute attack action with Intimidate. When it resolves onto the combat
        chain, the opponent banishes a random card from hand to their Intimidate
        banish zone (the keyword is applied generically at card resolution).
        Tests: pack_hunt_has_intimidate,
               pack_hunt_intimidates_on_resolve,
               test_pack_hunt_intimidate_banishes_opponent_card

  [x] Smash Instinct (Card::SmashInstinctY)
        Brute attack action with Intimidate. Shares the resolution-time Intimidate
        with Pack Hunt: the opponent banishes a random card from hand when it
        resolves onto the combat chain.
        Tests: smash_instinct_has_intimidate,
               smash_instinct_intimidates_on_resolve

  [x] Wrecking Ball (Card::WreckingBallR)
        Brute attack action. On play, draw a card then discard a card; if the
        discarded card has 6 or more power, Intimidate (the ConditionalIntimidate
        on-play effect on the shared DrawDiscardHit6 condition). Under Rhinar a
        6-power discard Intimidates twice — once from his OnDiscard6Intimidate
        constant ability (fired inside the discard) and once from this effect.
        Tests: wrecking_ball_discarding_power6_intimidates_twice_under_rhinar,
               wrecking_ball_discarding_below_power6_does_not_intimidate

  [x] Muscle Mutt  (Card::MuscleMuttY)
        Vanilla generic attack action (cost 3, 6 power, 2 defense). No rules
        text: the tests pin down that it is deliberately effectless and that it
        hits for exactly its printed power.
        Tests: muscle_mutt_is_a_vanilla_6_power_generic_attack

  [x] Raging Onslaught (Card::RagingOnslaughtY)
        Vanilla generic attack action (cost 3, 6 power, 3 defense). As above.
        Tests: raging_onslaught_is_a_vanilla_6_power_generic_attack

  [x] Smash with Big Tree (Card::SmashWithBigTreeY)
        Vanilla brute attack action (cost 2, 6 power, 0 defense) with no_block,
        so it cannot be used to block even while sitting in the defender's hand.
        Tests: smash_with_big_tree_is_a_vanilla_6_power_brute_attack,
               smash_with_big_tree_cannot_be_used_to_block

  [x] Clearing Bellow (Card::ClearingBellowB)
        0-cost brute action with Intimidate and Go Again. No on-play effect:
        both halves of the text are keywords the engine applies generically —
        Intimidate fires as the card resolves (here on its way to the graveyard
        rather than the combat chain) and Go Again spares the action point.
        Tests: clearing_bellow_has_go_again_and_intimidate,
               clearing_bellow_intimidates_on_resolve_and_keeps_the_action_point

  [x] Wounded Bull (Card::WoundedBullY)
        On play, if you have less health than an opposing hero, it gains +1
        power. The HasLessLife condition drives the shared ConditionalPower
        payoff: life totals are compared as the card resolves, and the +1 is
        banked on the player and folded into the chain's power at combat damage.
        The comparison is strict (level on life grants nothing) and owner-
        relative, so the same totals that deny one player the bonus grant it to
        the other.
        Tests: wounded_bull_effect_is_conditional_power_on_less_life,
               wounded_bull_banks_plus1_when_behind_on_life,
               wounded_bull_banks_nothing_when_level_on_life,
               wounded_bull_banks_nothing_when_ahead_on_life,
               wounded_bull_condition_is_relative_to_its_owner,
               wounded_bull_hits_for_7_when_behind_on_life,
               wounded_bull_hits_for_6_when_ahead_on_life

  [x] Come to Fight (Card::ComeToFightB)
        Go Again. On play, the next attack action card you play this turn gets
        +1 power. The new NextAttackPower effect banks the bonus on the player
        as `next_attack_action_bonus`; it is the class-agnostic sibling of
        Awakening Bellow's brute-only bank, so an attack action card of any
        class takes it, while a weapon swing — not an attack action card —
        leaves it banked for a later attack action that turn.
        Tests: come_to_fight_effect_is_next_attack_power,
               come_to_fight_banks_plus1_for_the_next_attack,
               come_to_fight_followup_generic_attack_gets_plus1,
               come_to_fight_followup_brute_attack_also_gets_plus1,
               come_to_fight_weapon_swing_does_not_get_plus1,
               come_to_fight_bonus_does_not_survive_the_turn,
               come_to_fight_played_from_hand_banks_the_bonus_and_keeps_the_action_point

  [x] Sigil of Solace (Card::SigilofSolaceB)
        "Gain 1 life." A 0-cost instant with no power or defense, so it never
        attacks or blocks — it resolves to the graveyard, healing its owner on
        the way, and as an instant costs no action point. There is no maximum
        life total, so the gain applies even at full health.
        Tests: sigil_of_solace_effect_is_gain_1_life,
               sigil_of_solace_gains_1_life_on_resolve,
               sigil_of_solace_heals_only_its_owner
