  [x] Rhinar (hero)  (Card::Rhinar)
        Constant ability (OnDiscard6Intimidate): whenever Rhinar discards a card
        with 6 or more power, Intimidate (the opponent banishes a card from
        hand). Fires from both discard paths — the "discard a card" additional
        cost (Alpha Rampage, Wrecker Romp) and the play-effect draw-then-discard
        (Bare Fangs, Wild Ride). Independent of the played card's own keywords,
        so Alpha Rampage (Intimidate) discarding a 6-power card intimidates twice.

            Rhinar's own action phase. Only the turn player has an action phase,

            your own turn, so this changes nothing for them; the case it rules
            out is paying for Rally the Rearguard's ability while defending —
            the engine's only off-turn discard. The gate is in the trigger
            rather than at its call sites so it holds for any off-turn discard
            added later.

        Text-free blue defense reactions — Dodge blocks 2 for 0, Toughen Up
        blocks 4 for 2 — implemented together because what makes them work is
        the defense-reaction rules rather than any card-specific effect:
          * they are never declared as blockers in the defend step, whatever
            their printed defense (`can_be_declared_as_blocker` excludes the
            card type, not just the `no_block` flag, so the rule holds for a
            future defense reaction that doesn't carry it);
          * only the defender is offered them, and only in the defend reaction
            step, where they go on the stack like an instant and can be
            responded to before they resolve;
          * a cost is paid by pitching in the reaction window's own pitch phase
            (ReactionPitch) — Dodge is free and commits straight to the stack,
            Toughen Up's 2 has to be pitched for;
          * as the reaction resolves it joins the chain link the attack sits on
            (`commit_defense_reaction`) instead of going to the graveyard, so
            its block adds to whatever was declared in the defend step, and it
            leaves for the graveyard with the blockers when the chain closes;
          * either can be played from the arsenal as well as from hand, on the
            same terms (see the NOTES on arsenal plays below).

  [x] Pack Call (Card::PackCallY)
        "When you defend with Pack Call, reveal the top card of your deck. If
        it has 6 or more power, put it on top of your deck. Otherwise, put it
        on the bottom." A 6-power brute attack action that also carries the
        catalog's first `defend_effect`, wiring up the previously unused
        `DefendEffect::Reveal6BottomOtherwise`.
          * The trigger is not applied where the card is committed. Blockers
            are declared first; when the defender finishes the defend step,
            `push_defend_triggers` puts a trigger on the stack for every
            declared blocker carrying a `defend_effect`, as one batch. Each
            then resolves as its own stack layer, so a trigger can be responded
            to like a played card.
          * Those layers resolve in a window of their own, `Phase::DefendTriggers`,
            which sits between the defend step and the ordinary `Reaction`
            window. When its stack empties it hands over to `Reaction` rather
            than dealing combat damage (see `close_priority_window`), so the
            reaction window still happens and damage waits for *it* to close.
            The window is only entered when there is at least one trigger to
            resolve, so an ordinary block costs no extra round of priority.
            `DefendPitch` is its pitch phase, returning there once a
            cost is covered — currently unreachable, since nothing playable in
            the window costs anything, but pinned by a test so the first costed
            instant does not land in the wrong window.
          * The stack entry is a `PendingCard` typed `ActionType::DefendTrigger`
            — a pseudo-action the engine pushes and `legal_actions` never
            offers. `resolve_top_of_stack` recognises it and resolves the
            effect alone: the card is already on the combat chain from when it
            was declared and stays there, so nothing changes zones, no action
            point is spent, and the card's keywords and on-play effect are not
            re-fired.
          * Simultaneous triggers resolve in the order their blockers were
            declared. The stack resolves top-down and `chain_link_indices`
            walks back through declaration order, so pushing in that order
            leaves the first-declared blocker's trigger on top. Real FaB lets
            the controller order them; there is no choice phase for that, and
            declaration order is one the player already expressed.
          * A blocker with no `defend_effect` — nearly all of them — puts
            nothing on the stack, so an ordinary block still costs no extra
            round of priority.
          * The trigger window is narrowed to instants for both players: an
            instant may be played and resolved above a pending trigger, a
            reaction — attack or defense — may not. Narrowing by phase rather
            than by scanning the stack means playing an instant in response
            cannot reopen the window underneath itself. Reactions are legal
            again in the `Reaction` window this one hands over to, so blocking
            with Pack Call no longer costs the defender their reaction window.
            Enforced where the engine enforces every other such rule, in
            `legal_actions`; `step` executes whatever it is handed.
          * "Your deck" is the deck of whoever defends with it, which is never
            the turn player. `reveal_top_keep_6` therefore works off the
            declaring player, and the attacker's deck is left alone.
          * The 6-power boundary is inclusive: exactly 6 stays on top. Below
            it, the card is detached and re-attached at the bottom, so the deck
            is reordered and never resized.
          * The reveal is public either way, so the card is left BothKnow — on
            the bottom that mirrors a face-up pitched card being bottomed (see
            `bottom_pitch_card`).
          * An empty deck is a legal state to resolve in (a decked player can
            still declare blockers), so it is a no-op rather than a panic.
        Tests: pack_call_is_a_6_power_brute_attack_with_a_defend_trigger,
               pack_call_trigger_waits_on_the_stack_instead_of_firing_on_declaration,
               pack_call_trigger_resolves_leaving_the_card_on_the_combat_chain,
               pack_call_keeps_a_power6_card_on_top,
               pack_call_bottoms_a_sub_power6_card,

            does count: it joins the chain link as it resolves, so a declared
            blocker plus a Dodge nullifies the bonus.

            made with, so a brute *weapon* swing takes it — Bone Basher both
            takes and spends it, where Awakening Bellow's and Come to Fight's

            bonuses read the chain differently.

            pay out: the attack it was banked for has happened, so a nullified
            bonus is gone rather than saved for the next one. A non-brute attack

  [x] Rally the Rearguard (Card::RallyTheRearguardB)
        "Once per turn Instant — Discard a card: Rally the Rearguard gains +3
        block. Activate this ability only while Rally the Rearguard is
        defending." A 2-cost blue generic attack action (4 power, 2 block) whose
        text is entirely an activated ability: the catalog's first ability that
        is neither an equipment piece's nor a weapon swing, and the first whose
        effect the engine actually applies (`apply_defending_ability`).
          * It is activated from the combat chain, a zone
            `get_equipment_activations` does not scan — that one walks the
            equipment and weapon slots. The new `get_defending_activations`
            finds a card carrying an `only_while_defending` ability on the
            active player's chain instead.
          * "While defending" is read as the two windows where a declared
            blocker is answering a live attack: `DefendTriggers` and `Reaction`.
            The phase gate is what does the work, because blockers stay on the
            chain until the action phase ends — a card that blocked an earlier
            attack this turn is still sitting there during a later instant
            window, no longer defending anything. Narrowing by phase is the same
            approach the defend-trigger window takes, and is tested with
            everything else saying yes so the gate is what is being pinned.
          * Only the defender is offered it: the turn player's chain holds their
            attack, not blockers, so the rule is that the active player is not
            the turn player. Attacking *with* Rally therefore offers nothing.
          * The activation goes on the stack while the card stays on the chain,
            still blocking — the same shape as a Pack Call defend trigger, and
            the reason `commit_pending_to_stack` skips its detach/relocate for a
            card activated while defending, and `resolve_top_of_stack` resolves
            the effect alone. Being on the stack, it can be responded to: an
            instant resolves above it before the +3 lands.
          * "+3 block" is granted to *that copy*, on the new
            `CardState::defense_bonus`, rather than banked on the player as the
            power bonuses are — two copies blocking the same attack are pumped
            independently. `chain_link_total` now takes the whole `CardState`
            rather than the printed `CardData` so the block total can count it.
          * The discard is a cost, paid as the activation goes on the stack (the
            same moment a played card's `AdditionalCostType::DiscardCard` is
            paid) and routed through `apply_discard_cost`. An empty hand cannot
            pay it, so the ability is not offered at all. It does *not* fire
            Rhinar's `OnDiscard6Intimidate`, even on a 6-power discard: his
            ability is action-speed and this discard is made while defending,
            on the opponent's turn — see the Rhinar entry above.
          * "Once per turn" is tracked per card on
            `CardState::ability_used_this_turn`, set when the activation is put
            on the stack — the cost is paid by then, so responding to it cannot
            hand the use back — and cleared for every card at `begin_turn`. The
            block bonus is cleared separately, as the card leaves the chain in
            `close_combat_chain`, so it lasts exactly as long as its combat.
          * The ability costs no action point (it is an instant), and the
            attacker's point is still spent by the attack as the window closes.
          * `DefendEffect::PitchToBlock` was put in the enum for this card before
            it was understood to be an activated ability. It is unused and no
            card carries it; the comment in `apply_defend_effect` now says so.

- Some shared combat mechanics (e.g. Intimidate) already have coverage in the
  general `fab_step::tests` module, but the cards above are not yet considered
  fully implemented + dedicated-card-tested and so remain on the TODO list.
  (This note used to cite "Pack Call token generation" as a second example.
  Pack Call is now complete, and its text in this catalog generates no tokens,
  so the example is gone rather than moved.)
- Arsenal plays: a card in the arsenal is offered as a play in the same windows
  a hand card would be (action, instant and reaction), gated by the same speed
  and action-point rules. It is paid for by pitching from hand, and because the
  arsenal card is not part of the hand's pitch pool, the whole hand can pay for
  it; it leaves the arsenal only once the cost is covered, freeing the slot to
  be refilled that turn. Tests: legal_actions_in_action_phase_offers_the_arsenal_card,
  legal_actions_in_action_phase_excludes_an_arsenal_card_of_the_wrong_speed,
  legal_actions_in_action_phase_pays_for_the_arsenal_card_out_of_hand,
  legal_actions_in_reaction_phase_offers_the_arsenal_card_to_the_defender_only
  (in `legal_actions::tests`), test_attack_action_played_from_arsenal_resolves_to_the_chain,
  test_arsenal_slot_freed_by_playing_can_be_refilled_the_same_turn
  (in `fab_step::tests`).

- When a card is finished, add its dedicated test(s) to
  environment/src/fab_step_card_tests.rs and move it up to the COMPLETE
  section.
